OpenBid Architecture

A paid web product for Canadian facilities and goods suppliers. It checks a tender package against the company's saved evidence, cites the page for every requirement, and keeps a radar and calendar of federal and municipal opportunities.

PART 1The system on one page

PART 2How a document check works

This is the headline feature and the part competitors do not show. Every requirement must carry a page and a quote that our own code has found in the document.

ResultWhen it is givenExample for a cleaning supplier
MetA confirmed, unexpired evidence item satisfies a numeric or yes/no rule.Tender needs $2M liability cover; saved certificate shows $5M, valid past closing.
GapConfirmed evidence exists and falls short, or has expired.WSIB clearance on file expired last month.
UnknownNo evidence on file, or the requirement cannot be tested by a rule."Three references for contracts of similar size."

PART 3How tenders get in

Facilities and goods work is bought mostly by cities, school boards and hospitals, which the federal feed does not cover. Four intake paths feed one shared tender table, each through its own adapter.

PathWhat it bringsStageWhat I verified
CanadaBuysFederal notices with categories, regions, dates and attachment links.Release 1Live file has 67 columns; new notices refresh every 2 hours. No contract value or clearance fields.
UploadAny tender package from any portal, for checking.Release 1Claude accepts PDFs up to 600 pages and 32 MB per request; larger packages are split.
Forwarded alertsThe customer's own alert emails from MERX, Biddingo, bids&tenders or Ontario Tenders, parsed into their private radar.Release 1Postmark inbound needs its Pro plan. The forwarding flow is not yet tested.
MERX collectorPublic listing rows: title, buyer, closing date, link. No documents.Release 2, gatedOpen-solicitation listings load without login and are not blocked by robots.txt. Terms of use not yet read.
bids&tenders collectorSame listing fields, per municipality.Release 2, gatedrobots.txt allows crawling with a 10-second delay. Page structure not yet confirmed.
Ontario Tenders Portal and Biddingo are excluded from collectors. The first blocks its application in robots.txt and requires a login; the second blocks automated agents. Both are covered through forwarded alerts instead.

Each collector must pass a gate before it is built: its terms of use allow it, it runs a week without breaking, and it has a kill switch and a zero-results alarm. A collector that fails is dropped without touching the rest of the system.

PART 5Rules the build must not break

The model extracts; code decides.No model output sets a Met or Gap result directly.
No quote, no claim.A requirement is shown as verified only when its quote is found on its page.
Unknown is the default.Missing data, failed parsing and model refusals all end as Unknown or a failed check.
Never say "eligible".The product reports evidence against requirements. It does not promise qualification or award.
Every private row has an owner.Each customer table carries an organization ID with a database policy and a test.
Document text stays out of third parties.Nothing from a customer file goes to logs, analytics, error reports or job payloads.
The official source wins.Every tender links to its original notice, and deadlines say "verify before submitting".
Spend is capped.Each plan has a monthly check allowance, and the whole system has a hard AI budget.
